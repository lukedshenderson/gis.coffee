Convert your layer to another format.

CSV includes latitude/longitude (the centroid for lines and polygons) and a WKT geometry column.

Supported formats
  • Shapefile: zip the .shp, .shx, .dbf and .prj together
  • KML / KMZ: Google Earth files
  • GeoJSON / JSON: including Esri JSON
  • CSV: needs latitude/longitude or X/Y columns
  • GeoPackage: vector layers (.gpkg)
  • File Geodatabase: not readable in a browser; export it to one of the formats above
